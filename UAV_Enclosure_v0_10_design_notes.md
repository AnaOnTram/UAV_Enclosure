# UAV Enclosure v0.10

## Consolidated cable opening

- The two GPS cable holes and the separate LD06 JST hole were removed.
- One nominal 10 x 60 mm vertical service slot now carries all three cables.
- Slot center: approximately X = -4.5 mm, Y = 0 mm.
- The four slot corners have a 4.5 mm radius to reduce stress concentration and cable abrasion.

## GPS layout

- Both diameter 55.5 mm GPS pockets were shifted 5 mm to the right.
- New pocket centers:
  - X = +30 mm, Y = -29.5 mm.
  - X = +30 mm, Y = +29.5 mm.
- This creates a dedicated cable-routing channel between the GPS antennas and LD06 platform.

## Weight and streamlining changes

- Eight brim reinforcement pads were reduced from diameter 10 x 2.5 mm to diameter 8 x 2 mm.
- The M3 clearance holes and their mounting centers are unchanged.
- The reduced pads decrease edge protrusion while retaining 2.3 mm of radial material around each diameter 3.4 mm hole.
- The consolidated slot removes multiple exposed circular penetrations and reduces potential water-entry paths.
- GPS antennas remain recessed and the LD06 platform remains inside the canopy outline.
- Solid volume is 212.341 cm3, approximately 1.893 cm3 lower than v0.9.

## Geometry retained

- External brim corner radius: 30 mm.
- Hollow 136 x 146 mm center aperture.
- Reinforced perimeter collar.
- Eight v0.5-style brim mounting points.
- Rotated PiBracket-style LD06 fixing columns.

## Validation

- SolidWorks rebuild: successful.
- Solid body count: 1.
- Overall height: 80.8 mm.
