# UAV Enclosure
The ultimate goal for the project is to design a 3D printed enclosure for the up layer of the ZD850 UAV.

## Goals
1. Light
2. Rigid
3. Easy to mount
4. Fast to fabric
5. Suits for 3D printing
6. Overall design should be streamlined for aerodynamics

## Specifications
1. The center part (120mm * 80mm) of the enclosure should be at least 50mm high to contains the Pixhawk 6X (centered) and a RF control board (on the left of the pixhawk 6x).
2. The upper surface of the enclosure should have mounting for the LD06 LiDAR as well as the cable hole for the UART cable. The mounting platform for the LD06 should be higher than others so ensure proper LiDAR detection.
3. There should be two cable on the rear side of the enclosure for the two GPS antenna.
4. Use external brim for the mounting and fixation of the enclosure
5. Fighter canopy liked shape

## References
1. `ZD850_UP.stl` is the 3D modeling of the upper board of the ZD850 UAV where the enclosure should be attached to.
2. `LD06/` directory contains the solidworks 3d files for the LD06 lidar.
3. Each GPS antenna is circular shaped (burger bread liked) antenna with the diameters of 55mm.
