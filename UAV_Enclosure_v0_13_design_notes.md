# UAV Enclosure v0.13 — Tall, Single-Pocket LD06 Pedestal

## LD06 pedestal

- Pedestal height: 20 mm
- Increase from v0.11: 12 mm
- Pedestal top elevation: 60 mm from the mounting surface
- PiBracket-style locating-column height: 11.8 mm
- Maximum enclosure height including the columns: 71.8 mm

## Lightweight construction

- Replaced the two separate underside lightening pockets from v0.12 with one 44 mm × 44 mm pocket.
- Pocket depth: 20 mm, opening into the main electronics cavity.
- Remaining pedestal top skin: 3 mm.
- Approximate outer pedestal wall: 2 mm minimum.
- Two Ø11 mm circular reinforcement islands are retained directly below the diagonal LD06 locating columns.
- The pocket remains one connected volume; the circular islands provide local thickness below the mounting points.

## Validation

- Solid bodies: 1
- Rebuild status: successful
- Bounding box: 180.5 mm × 190.0 mm × 71.8 mm
- Solid volume: 171.696 cm³
- Surface area: 0.113832 m²
- v0.11 solid volume: 179.337 cm³
- Despite raising the LD06 interface by 12 mm, v0.13 uses about 7.64 cm³ less solid material than v0.11.

The available UAV reference geometry does not define the final propeller scan-plane elevation. The 12 mm LiDAR-interface increase is therefore a conservative provisional clearance; it should be checked against the assembled motor and propeller height.
