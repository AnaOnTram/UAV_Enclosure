# UAV Enclosure v0.7

## Retained shape

- Based directly on the v0.5 octagonal drafted canopy.
- Canopy draft: 10 degrees.
- External base: 180 x 190 mm with rounded corners.

## Structural floor and drone fixation

- The former open brim is filled with a continuous 3 mm floor.
- The four gaps between the canopy and the external base are completely closed.
- The former eight arm-hole fasteners and reinforcement pads were removed.
- New fixation uses the central slot pattern measured from `ZD850_UP.stl`:
  - Four diameter 4.5 mm through-holes at X/Y = +/-18.75 mm.
  - Four diameter 9 mm x 1.5 mm-deep recessed screw seats.
  - Central diameter 20 mm passage retained.
